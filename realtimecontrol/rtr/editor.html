how MIDI works

MIDI is a way to connect the robot to music and to external controllers (a MIDI keyboard, a foot pedal, a box of buttons…). There are two sides, pick the one you want:

out — the robot plays: it sends sounds to a MIDI device (speaker, music box, lights) as it moves through zones and actions.
in — you control the robot: you press a key/button on your MIDI controller and the robot reacts (change zone, change action, etc.).
Example: connect a MIDI keyboard, open the in tab, click learn next to next_zone, then press a key on the keyboard. From now on that key moves the robot to the next zone.

global

The device the robot's sounds go to, and the channel they're sent on.

zone

Pick a zone to set the sound the robot plays there. Each action in the zone can also have its own sound (played when the action starts, and at each pose).

global navigation

Commands that work from anywhere, no matter which zone the robot is in. learn = click it, then press a key on your MIDI controller — the robot remembers that key.

zones (per-zone input)

Keys that activate a specific zone or action. The command key moves the robot to that zone; each action's key triggers just that action.

live monitor

The last raw MIDI traffic: in = messages received on the MIDI port, out = commands the core sent. Newest at the bottom.
